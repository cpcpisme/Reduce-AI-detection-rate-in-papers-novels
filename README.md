## 项目简介 / Overview

本项目围绕中文论文、微信公众号文章、长篇小说和其他长文本场景，提供结构化润色与表达重构思路，重点关注句式节奏、段落衔接、词汇多样性和上下文一致性，而不是单纯进行机械同义词替换。

The project focuses on structured revision for Chinese papers, WeChat articles, novels, and other long-form text. It emphasizes sentence rhythm, paragraph transitions, lexical variety, and contextual consistency instead of mechanical synonym substitution.

## 主要方向 / Main Capabilities

- 论文与研究报告的语言润色 / Academic and report-oriented language refinement
- 长文本的句式、段落与表达结构重构 / Sentence, paragraph, and expression restructuring
- 重复表达与模板化措辞的识别和改写 / Identification and revision of repetitive or templated wording
- 小说与叙事文本的风格连续性维护 / Style continuity for novels and narrative text
- 公众号标题、导语、互动结构与推流数据对比 / WeChat title, intro, interaction, and distribution comparison
- 分段处理建议与场景化 emoji 使用 / Segmented processing and context-aware emoji use

## 使用建议 / Recommended Use

1. 先选择场景：论文、公众号文章或小说。
2. **按自然段、小节或场景分段提交，不要一次性提交全文。** 一次提交过长会影响上下文重点和改写效果。
3. 论文补充章节目标，公众号补充目标读者和发布目的，小说补充人物关系与叙事视角。
4. 公众号可按需加入 📌✨👉 等 emoji；论文通常不使用，小说按作品风格决定。
5. 公众号发布后记录改写前后的首次推流、阅读、点赞、分享和完读等后台数据，不填入未经验证的数字。

1. Choose a use case: paper, WeChat article, or novel.
2. **Submit one paragraph, section, or scene at a time instead of the full manuscript.** A single large submission can hurt context focus and revision quality.
3. Add context: section goals for papers, target audience and publishing intent for WeChat, and character relationships and point of view for novels.
4. WeChat articles may use emoji such as 📌✨👉 when appropriate; academic papers normally should not, while novels should follow their style.
5. After publishing a WeChat article, record backend metrics such as initial distribution, reads, likes, shares, and completion before and after revision. Do not invent unverified figures.
